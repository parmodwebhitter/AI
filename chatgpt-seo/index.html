ChatGPT SEO basics

What Is ChatGPT SEO? The Simple Explanation.

ChatGPT SEO, also called ChatGPT optimization, is the practice of making your business visible in ChatGPT's answers. It helps ChatGPT find, understand and trust your brand, so it names and links to you when buyers ask about your category.

Google SEO helps you rank in a list of links. ChatGPT SEO helps you appear inside the one answer ChatGPT gives, with a link and a reason to choose you.

ChatGPT SEO focuses on four things:

  • Letting ChatGPT's crawlers read your websiteTechnical AI SEO
  • Writing pages that answer buyer prompts directlyAEO services
  • Earning mentions on sites ChatGPT trustsGEO services
  • Tracking which prompts name you, and which don'tAI visibility monitoring

FAQ

ChatGPT SEO FAQs

What is ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO is the work of making your business more likely to be named and linked in ChatGPT's answers. It combines technical access, clear content, structured data and mentions on sources ChatGPT trusts.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my brand?

No. No one controls what ChatGPT says. We improve the signals it relies on and show you, prompt by prompt, how your visibility changes each month.

How long does ChatGPT SEO take?

Technical fixes can show up in ChatGPT search within weeks. Changes driven by new mentions and content usually take 3 to 6 months.

Is ChatGPT SEO different from Google SEO?

They overlap. Good Google SEO helps, but ChatGPT weighs brand mentions, reviews and clear answers more than rankings and backlinks alone.

How do you measure results?

We track a fixed set of buyer prompts each month and report how often your brand is mentioned, cited or linked, compared with your competitors.
