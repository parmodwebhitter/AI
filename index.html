What is AI SEO?

AI SEO, also called AI search optimization, is the work of making your business visible in answers from AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. It covers your website, your content and how your brand is mentioned across the web, so AI tools understand, trust and recommend you.

How is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO aims to rank your pages in a list of search results. AI SEO aims to get your brand named or cited inside the answer itself. Good traditional SEO still helps, but AI tools also weigh brand mentions, clear facts and content that answers questions directly.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Most businesses see clear changes in AI mentions within 3 to 6 months, depending on competition, the current state of the website and how much trusted content already exists about the brand.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT rankings?

No. No one controls what an AI tool says, so anyone who guarantees placement is not being honest. What we do is improve every signal AI tools rely on, and track your mentions every month so you can see the progress.

Which AI platforms do you cover?

We cover ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Microsoft Copilot, with Claude coming soon. Each platform picks its sources differently, so we plan for each one separately.

Do you work with clients worldwide?

Yes. As an AI SEO company that works fully online, we work with businesses in any country and can optimize for several languages and markets. All work, calls and reports are handled online, so your location makes no difference.

How much does AI SEO cost?

Plans start from $499 per month, depending on how many platforms and markets you want to cover. The free audit shows where you stand today, so you can decide what to invest in. See our pricing page for full details.
